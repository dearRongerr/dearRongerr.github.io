## 摘要

We develop methodology for a multistage decision problem with flexible number of stages in which the rewards are survival times that are subject to censoring. We present a novel Q-learning algorithm that is adjusted for censored data and allows a flexible number of stages. We provide finite sample bounds on the generalization error of the policy learned by the algorithm, and show that when the optimal Q-function belongs to the approximation space, the expected survival time for policies obtained by the algorithm converges to that of the optimal policy. We simulate a multistage clinical trial with flexible number of stages and apply the proposed censored-Q-learning algorithm to find individualized treatment regimens. The methodology presented in this paper has implications in the design of personalized medicine trials in cancer and in other life-threatening diseases.

我们针对一种具有灵活阶段数的多阶段决策问题提出了一套方法，其中奖励为受到删失影响的生存时间。本文提出了一种新的适用于删失数据的 Q-learning 算法，该算法能够处理灵活数量的决策阶段。我们给出了该算法所学习策略的泛化误差有限样本界，并证明当最优 Q 函数属于近似空间时，由算法得到的策略其期望生存时间将收敛到最优策略的期望生存时间。我们模拟了一个具有灵活阶段数的多阶段临床试验，并应用所提出的删失 Q-learning 算法来寻找个体化治疗方案。本文提出的方法对于癌症及其他危及生命疾病中的个性化医疗试验设计具有重要意义。

## 1. Introduction.

In medical research, dynamic treatment regimes are increasingly used to choose effective treatments for individual patients with long-term patient care. A dynamic treatment regime (or similarly, policy) is a set of decision rules for how the treatment should be chosen at each decision time-point, depending on both the patient’s medical history up to the current time-point and the previous treatments. Note that although the same set of decision rules is applied to all patients, the choice of treatment at a given time-point may differ, depending on the patient’s medical state. 

在医学研究中，动态治疗策略（dynamic treatment regimes）越来越多地被用于长期患者护理中，为个体患者选择有效的治疗方案。动态治疗策略（或称策略 policy）是一组决策规则，用于在每一个决策时间点根据患者截至当前时间点的医疗历史以及既往治疗情况来选择治疗方案。需要注意的是，尽管对所有患者应用的是同一组决策规则，但由于患者的医疗状态不同，在某一特定时间点所选择的具体治疗方案也可能不同。

Moreover, the patient’s treatment plan is not known at the beginning of a dynamic regime, since it may depend on subsequent time-varying variables that may be influenced by earlier treatments and response to treatment. An optimal treatment regime is a set of treatment choices that maximizes the mean response of some clinical outcome at the end of the final time interval [see, e.g., Murphy (2003), Robins (2004), Moodie, Richardson and Stephens (2007)]. 

此外，在动态治疗策略开始时，患者的完整治疗计划通常是未知的，因为它可能依赖于后续随时间变化的变量，而这些变量又可能受到早期治疗以及对治疗反应的影响。因此，最优治疗策略被定义为一组治疗选择规则，其目标是在最终时间区间结束时，使某一临床结局的平均响应最大化［参见 Murphy（2003）、Robins（2004）、Moodie, Richardson and Stephens（2007）等］。

We consider the problem of finding treatment regimes that lead to longer survival times, where the number of treatments is flexible and where the data are subject to censoring. This type of framework is natural for cancer applications, where the initiation of the next line of therapy depends on the disease progression and thus the number of treatments is flexible. In addition, data are subject to censoring since patients can drop out during the trial.

我们考虑这样一个问题：寻找能够延长生存时间的治疗策略，其中治疗次数是灵活可变的，并且数据受到删失的影响。这种框架在癌症应用中是非常自然的，因为下一阶段治疗的开始取决于疾病进展情况，因此治疗的次数并不是固定的。此外，由于患者可能在试验过程中退出，因此数据会受到删失的影响。

For example, in advanced nonsmall cell lung cancer (NSCLC), patients receive one to three treatment lines. The timing of the second and third lines of treatment is determined by the disease progression and by the ability of patients to tolerate therapy [Stinchcombe and Socinski (2008), Krzakowski et al. (2010)]. We focus on mean survival time restricted to a specific interval, since in a limited-time study, censoring prevents reliable estimation of the unrestricted mean survival time [see discussion in Karrison (1997), Zucker (1998), Chen and Tsiatis (2001); see also Wahed and Tsiatis (2006) in the context of sequential decision problems and see Robins, Orellana and Rotnitzky (2008) for an alternative approach].

例如，在晚期非小细胞肺癌（NSCLC）中，患者通常接受一到三条治疗线。第二条和第三条治疗的时间点取决于疾病进展情况以及患者对治疗的耐受能力 [Stinchcombe and Socinski (2008), Krzakowski et al. (2010)]。我们关注限制在特定时间区间内的平均生存时间，因为在有限时间的研究中，删失数据会阻碍对无限制平均生存时间的可靠估计 [参见 Karrison (1997)、Zucker (1998)、Chen and Tsiatis (2001) 的讨论；在序列决策问题背景下也可参见 Wahed and Tsiatis (2006)；关于另一种方法可参见 Robins, Orellana and Rotnitzky (2008)]。

Finding an optimal policy for survival data poses many statistical challenges. We enumerate four. First, one needs to incorporate information accrued over time into the decision rule. Second, one needs to avoid treatments which appear optimal in the short term but may lead to poor final outcome in the long run. Third, the data are subject to censoring since some of the patients may be lost to follow-up and the final outcome of those who reached the end of the study alive is unknown. Fourth, the number of decision points (i.e., treatments) and the timing of these decision points can be different for different patients. This follows since the number of treatments and duration between treatments may depend on the medical condition of the patient. In addition, in the case of a patient’s death, treatment is stopped. The first two challenges are shared with general multistage decision optimization [Lavori and Dawson (2004), Moodie, Richardson and Stephens (2007)]. The latter two arise naturally in the context of optimizing survival time, but are applicable to other scenarios as well. Developing valid methodology for estimating dynamic treatment regimes in this flexible timing setup is crucial for applications in cancer and in other diseases where such structure is the norm and appropriate existing methods are unavailable.

为生存数据寻找最优策略在统计上面临诸多挑战，我们总结为四点。

- 首先，需要将随时间累积的信息纳入决策规则之中。
- 其次，需要避免那些在短期内看似最优、但从长期来看可能导致较差最终结果的治疗方案。
- 第三，由于部分患者可能失访，且在研究结束时仍存活患者的最终结局未知，因此数据存在删失问题。
- 第四，不同患者的决策点（即治疗次数）及这些决策点的时间安排可能不同。这是因为治疗次数以及治疗之间的间隔可能依赖于患者的病情状况
- 此外，当患者死亡时，治疗也会随之停止。
- 前两个挑战与一般的多阶段决策优化问题是共通的 [Lavori and Dawson (2004), Moodie, Richardson and Stephens (2007)]；
- 而后两个挑战则在生存时间优化问题中自然出现，但同样也适用于其他应用场景。
- 在这种具有灵活时间结构的设定下，发展用于估计动态治疗策略的有效方法，对于癌症及其他具有类似结构的疾病应用至关重要，而现有方法在这些问题上往往尚不适用。

One of the primary tools used in developing dynamic treatment regimes is Q-learning [Murphy et al. (2007), Zhao, Kosorok and Zeng (2009), Laber et al. (2010), Zhao et al. (2011)]. Q-learning [Watkins (1989), Watkins and Dayan (1992)], which is reviewed in Section 2, is a reinforcement learning algorithm. Since we do not assume that the problem is Markovian, we present a version of Q-learning that uses backward recursion. The backward recursion used by Q-learning addresses the first two challenges posed above: it enables both accrual of information and incorporation of long-term treatment effects. However, when the number of stages is flexible, and censoring is introduced, it is not clear how to implement backward recursion. Indeed, finding the optimal treatment at the last stage is not well defined, since the number of stages is patient-dependent. Also, it is not clear how to utilize the information regarding censored patients.

构建动态治疗策略的主要工具之一是 Q-learning [Murphy et al. (2007), Zhao, Kosorok and Zeng (2009), Laber et al. (2010), Zhao et al. (2011)]。Q-learning [Watkins (1989), Watkins and Dayan (1992)] 是一种强化学习算法，其内容将在第2节中进行回顾。

由于我们不假设该问题具有马尔可夫性，因此我们给出了一种基于向后递推（backward recursion）的 Q-learning 版本。

Q-learning 中的向后递推机制可以解决上述前两个挑战：它既能够累积随时间获得的信息，又能够纳入长期治疗效果。

然而，当阶段数是灵活可变的且引入删失时，如何实现向后递推并不明确。事实上，由于阶段数依赖于患者个体，最后一个阶段的最优治疗并没有明确定义。此外，如何有效利用删失患者的信息也尚不清楚。

In this paper we present a novel Q-learning algorithm that takes into account the censored nature of the observations using inverse-probability-ofcensoring weighting [see Robins, Rotnitzky and Zhao (1994); see also Wahed and Tsiatis (2006), Robins, Orellana and Rotnitzky (2008) in the context of sequential decision problems]. We provide finite sample bounds on the generalization error of the policy learned by the algorithm, that is, bounds on the average difference in expected survival time between the optimal dynamic treatment regime and the dynamic treatment regime obtained by the proposed Q-learning algorithm. 

在本文中，我们提出了一种新的 Q-learning 算法，该算法通过逆删失概率加权（inverse-probability-of-censoring weighting, IPCW）方法来考虑观测数据的删失特性 [参见 Robins, Rotnitzky and Zhao (1994)；在序列决策问题背景下也可参见 Wahed and Tsiatis (2006)、Robins, Orellana and Rotnitzky (2008)]。我们给出了该算法所学习策略的泛化误差的有限样本界，即对最优动态治疗策略与由所提出的 Q-learning 算法得到的动态治疗策略之间在期望生存时间上的平均差异所建立的上界。

We also present a simulation study of a sequential-multiple-assignment randomized trial (SMART) [see Murphy (2005a) and references therein] with flexible number of stages depending on disease progression and failure event timing. 

We demonstrate that the censored-Q-learning algorithm proposed here can find treatment strategies tailored to each patient which are better than any fixed-treatment sequence. We also demonstrate the result from ignoring censored observations.

我们还对一个序贯多次随机分配试验（sequential-multiple-assignment randomized trial，SMART）[参见 Murphy (2005a) 及其中的相关文献]进行了模拟研究，该试验具有灵活的阶段数，具体取决于疾病进展情况以及失效事件发生的时间。我们证明，本文提出的删失 Q-learning 算法能够为每一位患者找到个体化的治疗策略，其效果优于任何固定的治疗序列。此外，我们还展示了忽略删失观测所带来的结果影响。

One general contribution of the paper is the development of a methodology for solving backward recursion when the number and timing of stages are flexible. As mentioned previously, this is crucial for applications but has not been addressed previously. In Section 4 we present an auxiliary multistage decision problem that has a fixed number of stages. Since the number of stages is fixed for the auxiliary problem, backward recursion can be used in order to estimate the decision policy. We then show how to translate the original problem to the auxiliary one and obtain the surprising conclusion that results obtained for the auxiliary problem can be translated into results regarding the original problem with flexible number and timing of stages.

本文的一个总体贡献在于：当阶段数及其时间安排是灵活可变时，提出了一种用于求解向后递推（backward recursion）的方法。

正如前文所述，这一问题在实际应用中至关重要，但此前尚未得到解决。

在第4节中，我们引入了一个辅助的多阶段决策问题，该问题具有固定的阶段数。

由于该辅助问题的阶段数是固定的，因此可以使用向后递推方法来估计决策策略。

随后，我们说明如何将原始问题转化为该辅助问题，并得出一个令人意外的结论：针对辅助问题得到的结果，可以被转换并应用到原始具有灵活阶段数与灵活时间安排的问题中。

An additional contribution of the paper is the universal consistency proof for the algorithm performance. Universal consistency of an algorithm means that for every distribution function on the sample space, the expected loss of the function learned by the algorithm converges in probability to the infimum of the expected loss, where the infimum is taken over all the measurable functions [see, e.g., Steinwart and Christmann (2008)]. 

本文的另一项贡献是对算法性能给出了普适一致性（universal consistency）的证明。

算法的普适一致性意味着：对于样本空间上的任意分布函数，由该算法学习得到的函数的期望损失，在概率意义下收敛到期望损失的下确界，其中该下确界是在所有可测函数中取到的 [参见 Steinwart and Christmann (2008)]。

In Section 6 we prove that when the optimal Q-functions belong to the corresponding approximation spaces considered by the algorithm, the algorithm is universally consistent. The proof presented here is algorithm-specific, but the tools used in the proof are widely applicable for universal consistency proofs when the data are subject to censoring [see, e.g., Goldberg and Kosorok (2012)]. While other learning algorithms were suggested for survival data [see, e.g., Biganzoli et al. (1998), Shivaswamy, Chu and Jansche (2007), Shim and Hwang (2009); see also Zhao et al. (2011) in the context of a multistage decision problem], we are not aware of any other universal consistency proof for survival data.

在第6节中，我们证明了当最优 Q 函数属于该算法所考虑的相应近似空间时，该算法是普适一致的（universally consistent）。这里给出的证明是针对该算法特定的，但其所使用的工具在处理删失数据时，对于普适一致性的证明具有广泛的适用性 [例如 Goldberg and Kosorok (2012)]。

尽管已有其他针对生存数据提出的学习算法 [参见 Biganzoli et al. (1998), Shivaswamy, Chu and Jansche (2007), Shim and Hwang (2009)；在多阶段决策问题背景下也可参见 Zhao et al. (2011)]，但据我们所知，目前尚无其他针对生存数据的普适一致性证明结果。

The paper is organized as follows. 

In Section 2 we review the Q-learning algorithm and discuss the challenges for adapting the Q-learning methodology for a framework with flexible number of stages and censored data. We also review existing methods for finding optimal policies. Definitions and notation are presented in Section 3. The auxiliary problem is presented in Section 4. The censored-Q-learning algorithm is presented in Section 5. The main theoretical results are presented in Section 6. In Section 7 we present a multistage-randomized-trial simulation study. Concluding remarks appear in Section 8. Supplementary proofs are provided in the Appendix. A description of and link to the code and data sets used in Section 7 appear in the supplementary material [Goldberg and Kosorok (2012)].

- 第3节给出相关定义与符号说明。

## 2. Q-learning.

### 2.1. Reinforcement learning. 

Reinforcement learning is a methodology for solving multistage decision problems. It involves recording sequences of actions, statistically estimating the relationship between these actions and their consequences and then choosing a policy (i.e., a set of decision rules) that approximates the most desirable consequence based on the statistical estimation. A detailed introduction to reinforcement learning can be found in Sutton and Barto (1998).

强化学习是一种用于求解多阶段决策问题的方法。它通过记录一系列动作序列，统计性地估计这些动作与其结果之间的关系，然后基于该统计估计选择一个策略（即一组决策规则），使其尽可能逼近最优结果。关于强化学习的详细介绍可参见 Sutton and Barto (1998)。

In the medical context of long-term patient care, the reinforcement learning setting can be described as follows. For each patient, the stages correspond to clinical decision points in the course of the patient’s treatment. At these decision points, actions (e.g., treatments) are chosen, and the state of the patient is recorded. As a consequence of a patient’s treatment, the patient receives a (random) numerical reward.

在长期患者护理的医学背景下，强化学习的设定可以描述如下：对于每一位患者，各个阶段对应其治疗过程中的临床决策点。在这些决策点上，需要选择动作（例如治疗方案），并记录患者的状态。作为治疗的结果，患者会获得一个（随机的）数值型奖励。

More formally, consider a multistage decision problem with $T$ decision points. Let $S_t$ be the (random) state of the patient at stage $t \in \{1, . . . , T + 1\}$ and let $S_t = \{S_1, . . . , S_t\}$ be the vector of all states up to and including stage $t$. Similarly, let $A_t$ be the action chosen in stage $t$, and let $A_t = \{A_1, . . . , A_t\}$ be the vector of all actions up to and including stage $t$. We use the corresponding lower case to denote a realization of these random variables and random vectors. Let the random reward be denoted $R_t = r(S_t, A_t, S_{t+1})$, where $r$ is an (unknown) time-dependent deterministic function of all states up to stage $t + 1$ and all past actions up to stage t. A trajectory is defined as a realization of $(S_{T +1}, A_T , R_T )$. Note that we do not assume that the problem is Markovian. In the medical context example, a trajectory is a record of all the patient covariates at the different decision points, the treatments that were given, and the medical outcome in numerical terms.

更正式地，考虑一个具有 $T$ 个决策点的多阶段决策问题。

令 $S_t$ 表示患者在第 $t \in \{1, . . . , T + 1\}$ 个阶段的（随机）状态，并令 $S_t = \{S_1, . . . , S_t\}$ 表示截至并包括第 $t$ 个阶段的所有状态向量。同样，令 $A_t$ 表示第 $t$ 个阶段所选择的动作，并令 $A_t = \{A_1, . . . , A_t\}$ 表示截至并包括第 $t$ 个阶段的所有动作向量。我们使用相应的小写字母表示这些随机变量及随机向量的一个实现。设随机奖励表示为 $R_t = r(S_t, A_t, S_{t+1})$，其中 $r$ 是一个（未知的）时间相关确定性函数，它依赖于截至第 $t+1$ 阶段的所有状态以及截至第 $t$ 阶段的所有历史动作。一个轨迹被定义为 $(S_{T +1}, A_T , R_T )$ 的一个实现。需要注意的是，我们并不假设该问题满足马尔可夫性。在医学背景的例子中，一个轨迹记录了患者在不同决策点的所有协变量、所给予的治疗，以及以数值形式表示的医学结局。

We define a policy, or similarly, a dynamic treatment regime, to be a set of decision rules. More formally, define a policy $\pi$ to be a sequence of deterministic decision rules, $\{\pi_1, . . . , \pi_T \}$, where for every pair $(s_t, a_{t−1})$, the output of the tth decision rule, $\pi_t(s_t, a_{t−1})$, is an action. Our goal is to find a policy that maximizes the expected sum of rewards. 表示从第 $t + 1$ 个阶段到第 $T$ 个阶段的期望累计奖励和，其中截至第 $t + 1$ 个阶段的历史信息由 $\{s_{t+1}, a_t\}$ 给定，并且在之后采用最优策略 $\pi^*$。

Finding a policy that leads to a high expected cumulative reward is the main goal of reinforcement learning. Naively, one could learn the transition distribution functions and the reward function using the observed trajectories, and then solve the Bellman equation recursively. However, this approach is inefficient both computationally and memory-wise. In the following section, we introduce the Q-learning algorithm, which requires less memory and less computation.

寻找一个能够获得较高期望累计奖励的策略是强化学习的主要目标。一个朴素的方法是利用观测到的轨迹去学习状态转移分布函数和奖励函数，然后递归求解 Bellman 方程。然而，这种方法在计算和存储方面都效率较低。在下一节中，我们将介绍 Q-learning 算法，该算法在内存占用和计算开销方面都更为高效。

### 2.2. Q-learning.

### 2.3. Challenges with flexible number of stages and censoring.

2.3 灵活阶段数与删失问题带来的挑战

As discussed in the Introduction, our goal is to develop a Q-learning algorithm that can handle a flexible number of stages and that takes into account the censored nature of the observations. We face two main challenges. First, recall that the estimation of the Q-functions in (3) is done recursively, starting from the last stage backward. Thus, when the number of stages is flexible, it is not clear how to perform the base step of the recursion. Second, due to censoring, some of the trajectories may be incomplete. Incorporating the data of a censored trajectory is problematic: even when the number of stages is fixed, the known number of stages for a censored trajectory may be less than the number of stages in the multistage problem. Moreover, the reward is not known for the stage at which censoring occurs.

如引言中所述，我们的目标是提出一种能够处理阶段数灵活变化且能够考虑观测删失特性的 Q-learning 算法。我们面临两个主要挑战。

首先，回顾（3）中 Q 函数的估计是通过递归方式进行的，即从最后一个阶段开始向前递推。因此，当阶段数是灵活可变时，如何执行递推的初始步骤并不明确。

其次，由于删失的存在，一些轨迹可能是不完整的。如何利用删失轨迹的数据是一个问题：即使阶段数是固定的，对于一个删失轨迹而言，其已观测到的阶段数也可能少于多阶段问题中的总阶段数。此外，在发生删失的阶段，其对应的奖励也是未知的。

### 2.4. Review of existing approaches.

Finding optimal policies or optimal treatment regimes has been discussed extensively in other work. We discuss shortly some additional work that is related to the approach taken here. However, we are not aware of any other existing approaches that address simultaneously both censoring and flexible number of stages.

寻找最优策略或最优治疗策略已在其他研究中得到广泛讨论。我们在此简要介绍一些与本文方法相关的研究工作。然而，据我们所知，目前尚无其他已有方法能够同时处理删失问题和阶段数灵活可变的问题。

The approach closest to our proposal is the censored-Q-learning algorithm of Zhao et al. (2011). Zhao et al. considered a Q-learning algorithm for censored data based on support vector regression adjusted for censoring with fixed number of stages. A simulation study was performed to demonstrate the algorithm’s performance; however, the theoretical properties of this algorithm were not evaluated.

与我们方法最接近的是 Zhao 等（2011）提出的删失 Q-learning 算法。Zhao 等人针对删失数据，在阶段数固定的情况下，基于支持向量回归并结合删失调整，构建了一个 Q-learning 算法。该工作通过模拟研究验证了算法的性能，但并未对该算法的理论性质进行分析。

A general approach for finding optimal policies that uses backward recursion was studied by Murphy (2003) and Robins (2004) in the semiparametric context, and by Murphy (2005b) in the nonparametric context. These works do not treat flexible number of stages or censoring, and cannot be applied to the framework considered here without some adjustments.

Murphy（2003）以及 Robins（2004）在半参数框架下研究了利用向后递推求解最优策略的一般方法，而 Murphy（2005b）则在非参数框架下进行了研究。这些工作并未考虑阶段数的灵活性或删失问题，因此在不进行一定调整的情况下，无法直接应用于本文所考虑的框架。

Another approach for finding optimal policies was studied by Orellana, Rotnitzky and Robins (2010) [see also van der Laan and Petersen (2007), Robins, Orellana and Rotnitzky (2008)]. Orellana et al. considered dynamic regime marginal structural mean models [Robins (1999)]. In this approach, for each regime, one considers all trajectories that comply to the regime up to some point. The trajectories are then censored at the first time-point at which they do not comply to the regime. The contribution of the noncompliant trajectories is redistributed among compliant trajectories that have the same covariate and treatment history, using the inverse-probability-ofcensoring weighting. Advantages and disadvantages of this approach compared to the backward recursion approach mentioned above are discussed in Robins, Orellana and Rotnitzky (2008), Section 5. We note that it is assumed in their approach that the length of each stage is fixed, an assumption we do not require.

另一种用于寻找最优策略的方法由 Orellana、Rotnitzky 和 Robins（2010）研究提出［亦可参见 van der Laan and Petersen (2007)，Robins, Orellana and Rotnitzky (2008)］。Orellana 等人考虑了动态策略边际结构均值模型（dynamic regime marginal structural mean models）[Robins (1999)]。在该方法中，对于每一种策略，首先考虑所有在某一时间点之前遵循该策略的轨迹；随后，在轨迹首次不再符合该策略的时间点对其进行删失。对于不符合策略的轨迹，其贡献通过逆删失概率加权（inverse-probability-of-censoring weighting）方法重新分配给那些具有相同协变量与治疗历史的符合策略的轨迹。

与上述基于向后递推的方法相比，该方法的优缺点在 Robins, Orellana and Rotnitzky（2008）第5节中进行了讨论。需要指出的是，该方法假设每个阶段的长度是固定的，而这一假设在本文中并不需要。

This general issue is also related to the analysis of two-stage randomized trials involving right-censored data studied in a series of papers including Lunceford, Davidian and Tsiatis (2002), Wahed and Tsiatis (2006), Wahed (2009), Miyahara and Wahed (2010). The authors use inverse-probability-ofcensoring to correct for censoring. See also Thall et al. (2007) that considers analysis of two-stage randomized trials with interval censoring. However, the main focus of these works is in finding the best regime from a finite number of optional regimes, as opposed to the individualized-treatment policies addressed in our proposal.

这一一般性问题也与一系列关于含右删失数据的两阶段随机试验分析工作相关，包括 Lunceford, Davidian and Tsiatis (2002)、Wahed and Tsiatis (2006)、Wahed (2009)、Miyahara and Wahed (2010) 等。这些研究中，作者均采用逆删失概率加权（inverse-probability-of-censoring weighting）方法来校正删失的影响。另见 Thall et al. (2007)，其研究了含区间删失的两阶段随机试验分析问题。然而，这些工作的主要关注点在于从有限个备选策略中寻找最优策略，而不同于本文所研究的个体化治疗策略问题。

## 3. Preliminaries.

In this section we present definitions and notation which will be used in the paper.

Let $T$ be the maximal number of decision time-points for a given multistage time-dependent decision problem. Note that the number of stages for different observations can be different. For each $t = 1, . . . , T$ , the state $S_t$ is the pair $S_t = (Z_t, R_{t−1})$, where $Z_t$ is either a vector of covariates describing the state of the patient at beginning of stage $t$ or $Z_t = \emptyset$. $Z_t = \emptyset$ indicates that a failure event happened during the $t$-th stage which has therefore reached a terminal state.

设 $T$ 为给定多阶段时间依赖决策问题中的最大决策时间点数。需要注意的是，不同观测对应的阶段数可能不同。对于每个 $t = 1, . . . , T$，状态 $S_t$ 定义为一对 $S_t = (Z_t, R_{t−1})$，其中 $Z_t$ 是用于描述第 $t$ 阶段开始时患者状态的协变量向量，或者 $Z_t = \emptyset$。其中 $Z_t = \emptyset$ 表示在第 $t$ 阶段发生了失效事件，因此该阶段已进入终止状态。

 $R_{t−1}$ is the length of the interval between decision time-points $t − 1$ and $t$, where we denote $R_0 ≡ 0$. Although in the usual Q-learning context $\sum^t_{j=1} R_j$ is the sum of rewards up to and including stage $t$,  in our context it is more useful to think of this sum as the total survival time up to and including stage $t$. Let $A_t$ be an action chosen at decision time $t$, where $A_t$ takes its values in a finite discrete space $A$.

$R_{t−1}$ 表示第 $t − 1$ 个决策时间点与第 $t$ 个决策时间点之间的时间间隔长度，其中我们规定 $R_0 \equiv 0$。虽然在通常的 Q-learning 语境中，$\sum^t_{j=1} R_j$ 表示截至并包括第 $t$ 阶段的奖励总和，但在本文的背景下，更有意义的理解是将该和视为截至并包括第 $t$ 阶段的总生存时间。

令 $A_t$ 表示在第 $t$ 个决策时间点所选择的动作，其中 $A_t$ 的取值属于有限离散空间 $A$。

The model assumes that observations are subject to censoring. Let $C$ be a censoring variable and let $S_C(x) = P (C ≥ x)$ be its survival function. We assume that censoring is independent of both covariates and failure time. We assume that $C$ takes its values in the segment $[0, \tau ]$ where $\tau < \infty$ and that $S_C(\tau ) > K_{min} > 0$. Let $\delta_t$ be an indicator with $\delta_t = 1$ if no censoring event happened before the $(t + 1)$-th decision time-point. Note that $\delta_{t−1} = 0 \implies \delta_t = 0$.

该模型假设观测数据存在删失。令 $C$ 为删失变量，并令 $S_C(x) = P (C ≥ x)$ 为其生存函数。我们假设删失与协变量以及失效时间相互独立。同时假设 $C$ 的取值范围为区间 $[0, \tau ]$，其中 $\tau < \infty$，并且满足 $S_C(\tau ) > K_{min} > 0$。

令 $\delta_t$ 为指示变量，当在第 $(t + 1)$ 个决策时间点之前未发生删失事件时，$\delta_t = 1$。需要注意的是，若 $\delta_{t−1} = 0$，则必然有 $\delta_t = 0$。

**Remark 3.1.**  Note that for a censoring variable, we define the survival function $S_C(x)$ as $P (C \ge x)$ rather than the usual $P (C > x)$. This is because given a failure time $x$, we are interested in the probability $P (C \ge x)$. 

**备注 3.1.**

注意，对于删失变量，我们将生存函数 $S_C(x)$ 定义为 $P (C \ge x)$，而不是通常的 $P (C > x)$。这是因为在给定失效时间 $x$ 的情况下，我们更关注概率 $P (C \ge x)$。

However, to avoid complications that are not of interest to the main results of this paper, we assume that the probability of simultaneous failure and censoring is zero [see, e.g., Satten and Datta (2001)].

然而，为了避免引入与本文主要结果无关的复杂性，我们假设失效与删失同时发生的概率为零 [参见 Satten and Datta (2001)]。

The inclusion of failure times in the model affects the trajectory structure. Usually, a trajectory is defined as a $(2T + 1)$-length sequence $\{S_1, A_1, S_2, . . . , A_T , S_{T +1}\}$. However, in our context, if a failure event occurs before decision time-point $T$ , the trajectory will not be of full length. Denote by $\bar{T}$ the (random) number of stages for the individual $(\bar{T} \le T )$. Due to the censoring, the trajectories themselves are not necessarily fully observed. Assume that a censoring event occurred during stage t. Note that this means that $\delta_{t−1} = 1$  while $\delta_t=0$ and that $C < \sum^t_{i=1}R_i$. In this case the observed trajectories  have the following structure: $\{S_1, A_1, S_2, . . . , A_t\}$ and $C$ is also observed.

将失效时间纳入模型会影响轨迹的结构。通常，一个轨迹被定义为长度为 $(2T + 1)$ 的序列 $\{S_1, A_1, S_2, . . . , A_T , S_{T +1}\}$。

然而在本文的设定中，如果在第 $T$ 个决策时间点之前发生失效事件，则轨迹不会具有完整长度。记 $\bar{T}$ 为个体的（随机）阶段数（$\bar{T} \le T$）。

由于删失的存在，轨迹本身也不一定能够被完整观测。假设在第 $t$ 阶段发生了删失事件。需要注意的是，这意味着 $\delta_{t−1} = 1$ 而 $\delta_t = 0$，并且有 $C < \sum^t_{i=1}R_i$。

在这种情况下，观测到的轨迹结构为 $\{S_1, A_1, S_2, . . . , A_t\}$，同时删失时间 $C$ 也是可观测的。

## 4. The auxiliary problem.

In this section we construct an auxiliary Q-learning model for our original problem. The modified trajectories of the construction are of fixed length $T$ , and the modified sum of rewards is less than or equal to $\tau$ . We then show how results obtained for the auxiliary problem can be translated into results regarding the original problem.

（本节内容）

在本节中，我们为原始问题构建一个辅助的 Q-learning 模型。

（构造性质）该构造中的 **修改后轨迹（modified trajectories）** 具有固定长度 $T$，并且修改后的奖励总和小于或等于 $\tau$。

（结果传递）随后，我们说明如何将辅助问题中得到的结果，转化为关于原始问题的结果。

For the auxiliary problem, we complete all trajectories to full length in the following way. 

Assume that a failure time occurred at stage $t < T$ . In that case the trajectory up to $S_{t+1}$ is already defined. 

Write $S_j^{'} = S_j$ for  $1 ≤ j ≤ t+1$ and $A_j^{'} = A_j$ for $1 ≤ j ≤ t$. 

For all $t+1 < j ≤ T +1$ set $S_j = (\emptyset, 0)$  and for all $t + 1 ≤ j ≤ T$ draw $A_j$ uniformly from $\mathcal{A}$.

对于辅助问题，我们采用如下方式将所有轨迹补全为完整长度。

（情形假设）假设在第 $t < T$ 阶段发生了失效时间。在这种情况下，直到 $S_{t+1}$ 的轨迹已经被定义。

（变量定义）令 $S_j^{'} = S_j$，对于 $1 ≤ j ≤ t+1$，并令 $A^{'}_j = A_j$，对于 $1 ≤ j ≤ t$。

（补全规则）对于所有 $t+1 < j ≤ T +1$，设 $S_j = (\emptyset, 0)$，并且对于所有 $t + 1 ≤ j ≤ T$，从动作空间 $\mathcal{A}$ 中均匀随机抽取 $A_j$。

We also modify trajectories with overall survival time greater than $\tau$ in the  following way. 

Assume that $t$ is the first index for which $\sum_{t}^{i=1} Ri ≥ \tau$ .  

For all  $j \le t$, write $S_j^{'} = S_j$ and $A^{'}_j = A_j$ . 

Write $R^{'}_t = \tau − \sum^{t−1}_{i=1} R_i$ and assign $Z_{t+1}^{'} ≡  \emptyset$ and thus the modified state $S_{t+1}^{'} = (\emptyset, R_t^{'})$. 

If $t < T$ , then for all $t + 1 < j ≤  T + 1$ set $S_j = (\emptyset, 0)$ and for all $t + 1 ≤ j ≤ T$ draw $A^{'}_j$ uniformly from $\mathcal{A}$. 

The  modified trajectory is given by the sequence $\{S^{'}_1, A^{'}_1, S^{'}_2, . . . , A^{'}_T , S^{'}_{T +1}\}$. Note  that trajectories with fewer than $2T + 1$ entries and for which $\sum^t_{i=1} R_i ≥ \tau$  are modified twice. 

我们还以如下方式修改总生存时间大于 $\tau$ 的轨迹。

（情形假设）假设 $t$ 是满足 $\sum_{i=1}^{t} R_i \ge \tau$ 的第一个索引。

（保持已有部分不变）对于所有 $j \le t$，令 $S_j^{'} = S_j$ 且 $A^{'}_j = A_j$。

（截断奖励）令
$R^{'}_t = \tau − \sum^{t−1}_{i=1} R_i$，

并指定 $Z_{t+1}^{'} ≡  \emptyset$，因此修改后的状态为
$S_{t+1}^{'} = (\emptyset, R_t^{'})$。 

（后续轨迹补全）如果 $t < T$，则对于所有 $t + 1 < j ≤ T + 1$，设
$S_{t+1}^{'} = (\emptyset, R_t^{'})$；

## 6. Theoretical results.

Let $\{Q_1, . . . , Q_T \}$ be the approximation spaces for the minimization problems (13). Note that we do not assume that the problem is Markovian, but, instead, we assume that each $Q_t$ is a function of all the history up to and including stage $t$. Hence the spaces $Q_t$ can be different over $t$.

（函数空间定义）

设 $\{Q_1, . . . , Q_T \}$ 为最小化问题 (13) 的近似空间（approximation spaces）。

（非马尔可夫假设）

需要注意的是，我们并不假设该问题满足马尔可夫性（Markovian）。

相反，我们假设每一个 $Q_t$ 都是截至并包含第 $t$ 阶段的全部历史信息的函数。

（推论）

因此，不同阶段的空间 $Q_t$ 可以随 $t$ 而不同。

---

We assume that the absolute values of the functions in the spaces $\{\mathcal{Q}_t\}_t$ are bounded by some constant $M$ . 

（有界性假设）

我们假设空间 $\{\mathcal{Q}_t\}_t$ 中函数的绝对值均被某个常数 $M$ 所界定。

Moreover, we need to bound the complexity of the spaces $\{\mathcal{Q}_t\}_t$ . We choose to use uniform entropy as the complexity measure [see van der Vaart and Wellner (1996)]. 

（复杂度控制）

此外，我们还需要对空间 $\{\mathcal{Q}_t\}_t$ 的复杂度（complexity）进行约束。

我们选择使用一致熵（uniform entropy）作为复杂度度量 [see van der Vaart and Wellner (1996)]。

This enables us to obtain exponential bounds on the difference between the true and empirical expectation of the loss function that involves a random component, namely, the Kaplan–Meier estimator, as in (13) (see Lemma A.6). 

（采用 uniform entropy 的原因）

这种选择使我们能够对损失函数的真实期望（true expectation）与经验期望（empirical expectation）之间的差异建立指数界（exponential bounds）。

这里的损失函数包含一个随机成分（random component），即公式 (13) 中的 Kaplan–Meier estimator（Kaplan–Meier 估计量）（见 Lemma A.6）。

This is different from Murphy (2005b) who uses the covering number as a measure of complexity [Anthony and Bartlett (1999), page 148] for the squared error loss function.

（与已有工作的区别）

这一做法不同于 Murphy (2005b)。Murphy (2005b) 使用 covering number 作为平方误差损失函数（squared error loss function）的复杂度度量 [Anthony and Bartlett (1999), page 148]。

For every $\varepsilon > 0$ and measure $P$, we denote the covering number of $\mathcal{Q}$ by $N(\varepsilon, \mathcal{Q}, L_2(P))$, where $N(\varepsilon, \mathcal{Q}, L_2(P))$ is the minimal number of closed $L_2(P)$-balls of radius $\varepsilon$ required to cover $\mathcal{Q}$.

（覆盖数定义）

对于任意 $\varepsilon > 0$ 和测度 $P$，我们将 $\mathcal{Q}$ 的 covering number 记为
$N(\varepsilon, \mathcal{Q}, L_2(P))$。

（定义说明）

其中，$N(\varepsilon, \mathcal{Q}, L_2(P))$ 表示覆盖 $\mathcal{Q}$ 所需的最小闭 $L_2(P)$ 球（closed $L_2(P)$-balls）数量，这些球的半径均为 $\varepsilon$。

The uniform covering number of $\mathcal{Q}$ is defined as $\sup_P N(\varepsilon M, \mathcal{Q}, L_2(P))$ where the supremum is taken over all finitely discrete probability measures $P$ on $\mathcal{Q}$. 

（一致覆盖数定义）

$\mathcal{Q}$ 的一致覆盖数（uniform covering number）定义为
$\sup_P N(\varepsilon M, \mathcal{Q}, L_2(P))$，

其中上确界（supremum）是对 $\mathcal{Q}$ 上所有有限离散概率测度（finitely discrete probability measures）$P$ 取的。

（一致熵定义）

（假设条件）

我们对空间 ${\mathcal{Q}_t}$ 假设如下的一致熵界（uniform entropy bound）：
$$
