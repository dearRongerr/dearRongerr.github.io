==背景：== 

**论文研究的是：**① 动态治疗方案（DTRs）② 使用 强化学习（RL） ③ 尤其是 Q-learning ④ 应用于癌症等慢性病治疗决策

随着电子健康记录（EHR）的大规模积累，动态治疗方案（DTRs）成为智能医疗的重要研究方向。DTR 需要根据患者不同阶段的状态动态制定治疗策略，因此天然适合强化学习框架。

**核心问题：** 传统 Q-learning 在医疗 DTR 场景下存在：① 线性 Q-learning 表达能力差 ② 深度强化学习（Deep RL）计算成本太高 ③ 缺少严格理论保证 ④ 大规模 EHR（电子病历）数据难处理

① 线性 Q-learning 表达能力不足：传统线性模型难以刻画癌症治疗中的复杂非线性关系、高维状态特征和时序依赖问题，因此泛化性能有限。

② 深度强化学习计算代价高：Deep RL 虽然表达能力强，但存在参数规模巨大、训练不稳定、需要大量计算资源、医疗场景下可解释性差的问题；并且医疗 DTR 通常阶段数较少、安全要求极高、无法像游戏环境那种在线探索；因此很多 Deep RL 方法并不适合。

③ 缺乏严格的理论证明保证：很多 Deep RL 方法只能经验有效，无法证明泛化误差、收敛性、稳定性；而医疗领域非常重视理论可靠性。

④ 大规模医疗数据难以处理：电子病历数据量越来越大，传统 kernel 方法 $O(N^3)$ 的计算复杂度无法扩展到大规模数据。

**因此作者提出：** 构建一种“既能处理大规模数据、又有理论保证、同时计算效率高”的强化学习方法。

如何构建一种兼具非线性表达能力、计算效率与理论保证的强化学习方法，用于动态治疗方案（DTRs）？

==任务：==

⭐️ 非线性表达能力、计算复杂度、理论保证

作者的核心任务包括三部分：

**任务1：** 设计新的 Q-learning 框架；目标：①以线性模型表达能力强②比 Deep RL 更稳定 ③能处理连续状态空间 ④适合医疗 DTR

**任务 2：** 降低计算复杂度；目标：解决 kernel 方法 $O(N^3)$ 计算复杂度难以扩展的问题，需要支持大规模 EHR 和分布式训练

**任务 3：**  建立理论保证；目标：从统计学习理论角度证明①泛化误差界② 分布式学习不会损害性能 ③高维下依然有效

==核心方法：== 

方法 1：将 Q-learning 转化为监督学习，从 Bellman 方程出发，定义 $Q^*_t(x_t) = \mathbb{E}[Y^*_t|X_t]$ ，**从而** 将 Q-function 学习转化为多阶段最小二乘回归问题；**实现** 强化学习问题→监督学习问题

方法 2：使用 KRR（Kernel Ridge Regression），作者采用 RKHS + Kernel Ridge Regression，替代线性 Q-learning 和深度神经网络，

**核心优势：** 

| 方法            | 表达能力 | 训练复杂度 | 理论性 |
| --------------- | -------- | ---------- | ------ |
| 线性 Q-learning | 弱       | 低         | 强     |
| Deep RL         | 强       | 极高       | 弱     |
| KRR             | 强       | 中等       | 强     |

因此：KRR 成为折中方案。

方法 3：提出 DKRR（Distributed KRR）；作者采用分而治之的思想，将数据划分到多个节点 $D \to D_1,D_2,\ldots,D_m$

每个节点独立训练局部 KRR $Q_{D_{j,\lambda}} $ ，最后加权融合 $\bar{Q}=\sum_{j=1}^m \frac{D_j}{D}Q_{D_{j,\lambda}} $ 得到全局Q-function

方法 4：构建 DKRR-DTR；作者提出 DKRR-DTR 整体流程，实现数据划分→局部 KRR 训练→全局聚合→反向递推 Q-function→策略搜索

方法 5：建立理论分析框架；作者提出积分算子，定义 $L_K f(x)=\int f(x')K(x,x')dP(x')$ ，并利用 RKHS 理论、谱分析、统计学习理论，推导泛化误差界

得到核心结论：

1. 误差与维度无关
2. 分布式计算不会降低泛化能力；只要数据划分数量满足条件 $m(\log m+1) \le |D|^{\frac{2r+s-1}{4r+2s}}\log |D|$，则 DKRR 与单机 KRR 具有相同收敛速度

==实验结果与贡献：== 

论文通过两类癌症临床试验模拟验证方法有效性

实验 1：最大化生存时间；非小细胞肺癌（NSCLC），目标是提升生存时间，达到的结果：① DKRR-DTR 明显优于线性 Q-learning ② 接近 Deep RL 性能 ③ 训练时间远低于 Deep RL

实验2：最大化累计生存概率（CSP）；达到的结果：① Deep RL 略优 但 DKRR-DTR 更稳定 ② 计算代价更低，更适合离线医疗场景

复杂度结果：训练复杂度从 $O(N^3)$ 下降到 $ O(\frac{N^3}{m^2})$ ，说明分布式显著提升了可扩展性

==总结：== 

- 提升非线性表达能力
- 降低训练复杂度
- 支持大规模 EHR
- 提供理论保证
- 兼顾性能与效率

本文针对动态治疗方案中强化学习存在的 **表达能力不足** 与 **计算复杂度过高** 问题，提出了一种 **基于分布式核岭回归的 Q-learning 框架** ，通过将 **强化学习** 转化为 **多阶段监督学习问题** ，在保证 **泛化性能** 的同时显著降低了 **训练复杂度** ，并从统计学习理论角度建立了 **维度无关** 的泛化误差界。



## 摘要

In recent years, large amounts of electronic health records (EHRs) concerning chronic diseases have been  collected to facilitate medical diagnosis. 

Modeling the dynamic properties of EHRs related to chronic diseases  can be efficiently done using dynamic treatment regimes (DTRs). 

While reinforcement learning (RL) is a  widely used method for creating DTRs, there is ongoing research in developing RL algorithms that can  effectively handle large amounts of data. 

近年来，针对**慢性疾病**的大量 **电子健康记录（EHR）** 已被收集，用于辅助医疗诊断。对与慢性疾病相关的 EHR 的动态特性进行建模，可以通过 **动态治疗策略（DTRs）** 高效实现。虽然 **强化学习（RL）** 是构建 DTRs 的一种广泛使用的方法，但如何开发能够有效处理大规模数据的强化学习算法仍在持续研究中。

In this paper, we present a scalable kernel-based distributed Qlearning algorithm for generating DTRs. 

We perform both theoretical assessments and numerical analysis for  the proposed approach. 

本文提出了一种**可扩展的基于核函数的分布式 Q-learning 算法**，用于生成动态治疗策略。我们从理论分析和数值实验两个方面对所提出的方法进行了评估。

The results demonstrate that our algorithm significantly reduces the computational  complexity associated with the state-of-the-art deep reinforcement learning methods, while maintaining  comparable generalization performance in terms of accumulated rewards across stages, such as survival time  or cumulative survival probability.

实验结果表明，与当前最先进的深度强化学习方法相比，所提出的算法在**显著降低计算复杂度**的同时，在**各阶段累计奖励**（例如生存时间或累计生存概率）方面仍然保持了**可比的泛化性能**。

**Key words** : Q-learning, dynamic treatment regimes, distributed learning, kernel ridge regression

## 1. Introduction

Patients with chronic diseases, such as cancer, diabetes, and mental disease, usually undergo a  period of initial treatment, disease recurrence, and salvage treatment.

Explicitly specifying the  relationship of treatment type and drug dosage with patient response is generally difficult, so  practitioners have to use protocols in which each patient receives a similar treatment based on the  average responses of previous patients with similar cases. 

慢性疾病患者（如癌症、糖尿病和精神疾病）通常会经历**初始治疗、疾病复发以及挽救性治疗**等阶段。由于明确刻画**治疗类型和药物剂量与患者反应之间的关系**通常较为困难，临床实践中往往采用标准化治疗方案，即根据以往相似病例患者的平均反应，为每位患者提供相似的治疗。

However, illnesses respond ==heterogeneously  to treatment==. 

For example, a study on schizophrenia (Ishigooka et al. 2000) discovered that patients using the exact same antipsychotic experience drastically varied outcomes; 

some patients experience  few adverse events with improved clinical outcomes, whereas others have to discontinue treatment  due to worsening symptoms.

然而，疾病对治疗的反应具有明显的**个体异质性**。例如，一项关于精神分裂症的研究（Ishigooka 等，2000）发现，使用完全相同抗精神病药物的患者，其治疗结果存在显著差异；部分患者不良反应较少且临床症状得到改善，而另一些患者则因症状恶化不得不中止治疗。

 This has motivated researchers to advocate dynamic treatment regimes  (DTRs) to determine treatments dynamically and individually based on clinical observations of  patients (Murphy 2005a, Chakraborty and Moodie 2013, Almirall and Chronis-Tuscano 2016).

这一现象促使研究人员提出 **动态治疗策略（DTRs）** 的概念，即基于患者的临床观测信息，动态且个体化地制定治疗方案（Murphy 2005a；Chakraborty 和 Moodie 2013；Almirall 和 Chronis-Tuscano 2016）。

---

A DTR is defined by a set of sequential decision rules; each rule takes the clinical observations  of a patient at certain key points as input and returns the treatment action of doctors as output  (Tsiatis et al. 2019). 

动态治疗策略（DTR）由一组**序贯决策规则**构成；每条规则以患者在若干关键时间点的临床观测作为输入，并输出医生应采取的治疗行为（Tsiatis 等，2019）。

In this way, the design of a DTR can be considered a sequential decisionmaking problem and suits the reinforcement learning (RL) framework (Sutton and Barto 2018),  in which ==states, actions, rewards, and policies== correspond to the **clinical observations of patients**,  **treatment options**, **treatment outcomes**, and **series of decision rules**, respectively. 

因此，DTR 的设计可以被视为一个**序列决策问题**，这与强化学习（RL）框架天然契合（Sutton 和 Barto，2018）。在该框架中：

- **状态（states）** 对应患者的临床观测信息，
- **动作（actions）** 对应可选择的治疗方案，
- **奖励（rewards）** 对应治疗结果，
- **策略（policies）** 对应一系列决策规则。

RL simultaneously  addresses sequential decision-making problems with sampled, evaluative, and delayed feedback,  which naturally makes it a desirable tool for developing ideal DTRs.

强化学习能够同时处理**基于采样的、带评价的以及具有延迟反馈的序列决策问题**，因此自然成为开发理想动态治疗策略（DTRs）的一种有效工具。

---

Q-learning (Watkins and Dayan 1992) is a typical temporal-difference algorithm that produces a  sequence of high-quality actions in RL and has been substantially developed in the past decades  in health care (Oh et al. 2022). 

Q-learning（Watkins 和 Dayan，1992）是一种典型的**时序差分（temporal-difference）算法**，能够在强化学习中生成一系列高质量的决策动作，并且在过去几十年中已在医疗健康领域得到广泛发展（Oh 等，2022）。

Different from other applications, Q-learning for DTRs with a  finite horizon (typically small and not exceeding a few tens) commonly has the following important  characteristics:

与其他应用场景不同，**用于有限时域（通常较小且不超过几十步）的动态治疗策略（DTRs）的 Q-learning** 通常具有以下重要特征：

---

① Data structure: When a patient’s chronic disease is not effectively treated or managed through a  particular treatment plan, they may seek alternative treatment options from other doctors, resulting  in treatment records with limited duration. Additionally, in Q-learning for DTRs, doctors typically  have a limited number of treatment options to choose from based on patients’ treatment histories  (Tsiatis et al. 2019, Chap.1.4). 

**数据结构：** 当慢性疾病患者通过某一治疗方案未能得到有效治疗或控制时，他们可能会从其他医生处寻求替代治疗方案，从而导致治疗记录的持续时间有限。此外，在用于动态治疗策略（DTRs）的 Q-learning 中，医生通常会根据患者的既往治疗历史，从**有限数量的治疗选项**中进行选择（Tsiatis 等，2019，第 1.4 章）。

However, for a given treatment decision, the clinical observations of  patients can be unpredictable, resulting in an infinite number of states. This makes Q-learning for  DTRs distinct from classical tabular-based Q-learning, which focuses on finite actions and states  (Gosavi 2009). 

然而，对于某一给定的治疗决策，患者的临床观测具有不确定性，因此可能产生**无限数量的状态**。这使得用于 DTRs 的 Q-learning 不同于经典的基于表格（tabular）的 Q-learning，后者通常假设动作和状态空间均为有限（Gosavi，2009）。

To summarize, DTRs have finite horizons, and each stage of Q-learning for DTRs  has a finite number of actions but an infinite number of states.

综上，**DTRs 具有有限时域**，并且在用于 DTRs 的 Q-learning 中，**每个阶段的动作数量是有限的，但状态空间是无限的**。

---

② Task orientation: For DTRs with a small number of stages, like non-small-cell lung cancer  (NSCLC) patients typically undergoing one to three treatment lines (Socinski and Stinchcombe  2007), treatment decisions at each critical point in Q-learning are crucial for determining patient  outcomes, regardless of timing. 

**任务导向：** 对于阶段数较少的动态治疗策略（DTRs），例如非小细胞肺癌（NSCLC）患者通常只经历一到三线治疗（Socinski 和 Stinchcombe，2007），在 Q-learning 中，每个关键时间点的治疗决策对于患者最终结局都至关重要，与时间先后无关。

Hence, assuming one treatment phase is more critical than others  is inappropriate, and the standard Q-learning reward discounting method is unsuitable for such  DTRs.

因此，假设某一个治疗阶段比其他阶段更为重要是不合适的，而标准 Q-learning 中的**奖励折扣（reward discounting）方法**也不适用于这类 DTRs。

---

③ Quality guarantee: Quality is crucial in medical treatment, as even a small mistake may result  in extremely negative outcomes for patients. This necessitates not only a strong understanding  of treatment decisions but also solid theoretical guarantees supporting the effectiveness. 

**质量保证：** 在医疗治疗中，质量至关重要，因为即使是很小的错误也可能给患者带来极其严重的负面后果。因此，这不仅要求对治疗决策具有充分理解，还需要**坚实的理论保证**来支持方法的有效性。

This  requirement eliminates the use of some heuristic yet intricate designs of Q-learning (Oroojlooyjadid  et al. 2022) for generating DTRs.

这一要求使得一些**启发式但结构复杂的 Q-learning 设计**（Oroojlooyjadid 等，2022）不适用于生成动态治疗策略（DTRs）。

---

④ Computational costs: This increase in data size poses significant scalability challenges for the Q-learning algorithms, in terms of storage and computational complexity. This scalability requirement  excludes the popular deep learning in DTR since it inherently entails a substantial number of  parameters and hyperparameter, markedly escalating the computational resource requirements.

**计算成本：** 数据规模的增加在存储和计算复杂度方面为 Q-learning 算法带来了显著的可扩展性挑战。这种可扩展性需求在一定程度上限制了深度学习方法在 DTR 中的应用，因为深度学习通常包含大量参数和超参数，会显著提升计算资源的需求。

---

==The characteristics discussed above place strict limitations on the use of Q-learning for generating  DTRs. The need for quality guarantees excludes the commonly used linear Q-learning (Murphy  2005b), and the demands for computational efficiency and interpretability make the widely adopted  deep Q-learning (Oroojlooyjadid et al. 2022) unsuitable for DTRs.==  

上述特征对使用 Q-learning 生成动态治疗策略（DTRs）提出了严格限制。对**质量保证**的需求使得常用的**线性 Q-learning**（Murphy，2005b）难以满足要求；而对**计算效率和可解释性**的要求，则使广泛采用的**深度 Q-learning**（Oroojlooyjadid 等，2022）不适用于 DTRs。

In this paper, we aim to create  a scalable Q-learning algorithm that has solid theoretical guarantees for DTRs.

因此，本文旨在构建一种**具有坚实理论保证且具备可扩展性的 Q-learning 算法**，用于生成动态治疗策略。

---

### 1.1. Mathematical formulation of Q-learning for DTRs

DTRs often involve $T$ -stage treatment decision problems. 

Let $s_t \in \mathcal{S}_t$ be the pretreatment information  (or status) and $a_t \in \mathcal{A}_t$ be the treatment decision (or action) at stage $t$, where $\mathcal{S}_t$ and $\mathcal{A}_t$  are  sets of pretreatment information and treatment decisions, respectively. 

动态治疗策略（DTRs）通常涉及 **T 阶段的治疗决策问题**。

设$s_t \in \mathcal{S}_t$ 表示第 $t$ 阶段的**治疗前信息（或状态）**，
$a_t \in \mathcal{A}_t$ 表示第 $t$ 阶段的**治疗决策（或动作）**，

其中，$\mathcal{S}_t$ 和$\mathcal{A}_t$ 分别表示**治疗前信息集合**和**治疗决策集合**。

A history of personalized  treatment is generated in the form of $\mathcal{T}_T = \{s_{1:T +1}, a_{1:T} \}$, where $\mathcal{s}_{T +1}$ is the status after all treatment  decisions, $s_{1:t}$ and $a_{1:t}$ denote all historical status and actions recorded from stage $1$ to stage $t$, i.e., $s_{1:t} = \{s_1, s_2, \ldots , s_t\}$, $a_{1:t} = \{a_1, a_2, \ldots , a_t\}$. 

个体化治疗的历史可以表示为$\mathcal{T}_T = { s_{1:T+1}, a_{1:T} }$, 
其中，

- $ s_{T+1} $ 表示**所有治疗决策完成后的状态**； 
- $s_{1:t}$ 和 $a_{1:t}$ 分别表示从第 1 阶段到第 $t$ 阶段记录的**全部历史状态和动作**，即
  
  - $s_{1:t} = \{s_1, s_2, \ldots, s_t\}$
  - $a_{1:t} = \{a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_t\}$
    

Similarly, $\mathcal{S}_{1:t}$ and $\mathcal{A}_{1:t}$ represent the status space  and action space, respectively, formed from stage $1$ to stage $t$, i.e., $\mathcal{S}_{1:t} = S_1 × S_2 × \cdots × S_t$ and  $\mathcal{A}_{1:t} = A_1 × A_2 × \ldots × A_t$. 

类似地， $\mathcal{S}_{1:t}$  和  $\mathcal{A}_{1:t}$  分别表示从第 1 阶段到第  $t$  阶段形成的**状态空间**和**动作空间**，即

- $\mathcal{S}_{1:t} = \mathcal{S}_1 × \mathcal{S}_2 × \cdots × \mathcal{S}_t$
- $\mathcal{A}_{1:t} = \mathcal{A}_1 × \mathcal{A}_2 × \ldots × \mathcal{A}_t$ 

Let $R_t : (\mathcal{S}_{1:t+1}, \mathcal{A}_{1:t}) \to \mathbb{R}$ be the clinical outcome following the decision  $a_t$, which depends on the pretreatment information $s_{1:t+1}$ and treatment history $a_{1:t}$. 

设 $R_t : (\mathcal{S}_{1:t+1}, \mathcal{A}_{1:t}) \to \mathbb{R}$ 表示在第  $t$ 次决策之后的**临床结果**，该结果依赖于治疗前信息 $s_{1:t+1}$ 和治疗历史 $a_{1:t}$ 。 

In cancer  treatment, for example, $R_t$ can be set as the tumor size or the survival time when the $t$-th treatment  decision is made. Throughout the paper, we use capital letters ($A_1$, $A_2$, etc.) to denote random  variables and lowercase letters ($a_1$, $a_2$, etc.) to represent their realization.

例如，在癌症治疗中， $R_t$ 可以设定为进行第 $t$ 次治疗决策时的**肿瘤大小**或**生存时间**。

在本文中，我们使用**大写字母**（如$A_1$, $A_2$, 等）表示**随机变量**，使用**小写字母**（如 $a_1$, $a_2$, 等）表示其具体取值。

---

A DTR $\pi = (π_1, . . . , π_T )$, where  $\pi_t: \mathcal{S}_{1:t} \times \mathcal{A}_{1:t-1} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}_t$ , is a set of rules for personalized treatment  decisions at all $T$ stages. 

动态治疗策略（DTR）$\pi=(\pi_1,\ldots,\pi_T)$ , 其中 $\pi_t: \mathcal{S}_{1:t} \times \mathcal{A}_{1:t-1} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}_t$,

表示在全部  $T$  个阶段进行个体化治疗决策的一组规则。

The optimal DTR is that which maximizes the overall outcome of  interest $\sum_{t=1}^{T} R_t(s_{1:t+1}, a_{1:t})$ in terms of designing suitable treatment rules  $\pi=(\pi_1,\ldots,\pi_T)$ . 

最优 DTR 是通过设计合适的治疗规则 $\pi=(\pi_1,\ldots,\pi_T)$ 使总体关注的结果 $\sum_{t=1}^{T} R_t(s_{1:t+1}, a_{1:t})$ 达到最大。

Our  analysis is cast in a random setting to quantify the overall outcome (Murphy 2005b). 

为了量化总体结果（Murphy，2005b），本文在**随机环境**下进行分析。

Denote by   $\rho_t(s_t \mid s_{1:t-1}, a_{1:t-1})$  the transition probability of $s_t$ conditioned on $s_{1:t-1}$ and $a_{1:t-1}$. 

记 $\rho_t(s_t \mid s_{1:t-1}, a_{1:t-1})$ 为在给定历史状态 $s_{1:t-1}$  和历史动作  $a_{1:t-1}$  条件下，状态  $s_t$  的**转移概率**。

The value of a  DTR $\pi$ at stage $t$ is defined by

在阶段  $t$ 时，动态治疗策略  $\pi$  的价值（value）定义为：

$V_{\pi,t}(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}) = E_\pi\left[ \sum_{j=t}^T R_j(S_{1:j+1}, A_{1:j}) \middle| S_{1:t} = s_{1:t}, A_{1:t-1} = a_{1:t-1} \right]$

where $E_\pi$ is the expectation with respect to the distribution 

其中，$E_\pi$ 表示关于如下分布的期望：

\[ P_\pi = \rho_1(s_1) \mathbf{1}_{a_1 = \pi_1(s_1)} \prod_{t=2}^T \rho_t(s_t | s_{1:t-1}, a_{1:t-1}) \mathbf{1}_{a_t = \pi(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1})} \rho_{T+1}(s_{T+1} | s_{1:T}, a_{1:T}), \] 

and $\mathbf{1}_W$ is the indicator of the event $W$. 其中，$\mathbf{1}_W$  表示事件 $W$  的指示函数。



Denote the optimal value function of $\pi$ at stage $t$ as 

记策略  $\pi$ 在阶段  $t$ 的最优价值函数为

\[ V_t^*(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}) = \max_{\pi \in \Pi} V_{\pi,t}(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}), \] 

where $\Pi$ is the collection of all treatments. 其中，$\Pi$ 表示所有治疗策略的集合。

We aim to find a policy $\hat{\pi}$ to minimize $V_1^*(s_1) - V_{\hat{\pi},1}(s_1)$.

我们的目标是寻找一个策略  $\hat{\pi}$ ，使得以下差值最小：$V_1^*(s_1) - V_{\hat{\pi},1}(s_1)$


---

Let $p_t(a_t | s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1})$ be the probability that a decision $a_t$ is made given the history $\{s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}\}$. 

设 $p_t(a_t | s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1})$ 表示在给定历史信息 $\{s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}\}$  条件下作出决策 $a_t$ 的概率。

Define the optimal time-dependent $Q$-function by 

 定义最优的**时间相关 $Q$ 函数**为 
$$
 Q_t^*(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t}) = E\left[ R_t(S_{1:t+1}, A_{1:t}) + V_{t+1}^*(S_{1:t+1}, A_{1:t}) \middle| S_{1:t} = s_{1:t}, A_{1:t} = a_{1:t} \right], 
$$
where $E$ is the expectation with respect to the distribution $P := P_{T+1}$ and

其中，$E$ 表示关于分布 $P := P_{T+1}$ 的期望，并且

 \[ P_t = \rho_1(s_1) p_1(a_1 | s_1) \prod_{j=2}^t \rho_j(s_j | s_{1:j-1}, a_{1:j-1}) p_j(a_j | s_{1:j}, a_{1:j-1}). \]

---

Given the definition of $V_t^*$, it is easy to derive Murphy (2005b) 

根据 $V_t^*$  的定义，可以容易得到（Murphy，2005b）

\[ V_t^*(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}) = V_{\pi^*,t}(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}) = E_{\pi^*}\left[ \sum_{j=t}^T R_j(S_{1:j+1}, A_{1:j}) \middle| S_{1:t} = s_{1:t}, A_{1:t-1} = a_{1:t-1} \right], \] 

where $\pi^*$ is the optimal policy  and consequently 

其中，$\pi^*$ 表示最优策略。因此有

\[ V_t^*(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}) = \max_{a_t} Q_t^*(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t}), \tag{3} \]

showing that the optimal treatment decisions can be determined by maximizing the optimal $Q$-functions. 

这表明，最优治疗决策可以通过**最大化最优 $Q$ 函数**来确定。

▶︎

Thus, researchers have developed efficient algorithms for finding optimal $Q$-functions in the realm of RL (Murphy 2005b). 

因此，研究人员在强化学习（RL）领域中开发了多种用于寻找最优 (Q) 函数的高效算法（Murphy，2005b）。

With $Q_{T+1}^* = 0$, the definition of $Q_t^*$ indicates that 

当 $Q_{T+1}^* = 0$ 时，由 $Q_t^*$ 的定义可得
$$
 Q_t^*(s_{1:t}, a_{1:t}) = E\left[ R_t(S_{1:t+1}, A_{1:t}) + \max_{a_{t+1}} Q_{t+1}^*(S_{1:t+1}, A_{1:t}, a_{t+1}) \middle| S_{1:t} = s_{1:t}, A_{1:t} = a_{1:t} \right], \tag{4} 
$$
for $t = T, T-1, \dots, 1$. 其中 $t = T, T-1, \dots, 1$ 。

This property connects $Q$-functions with the well-known regression function (Györfi et al. 2006) in supervised learning as follows. 

该性质将 $Q$ 函数与监督学习中著名的**回归函数**（Györfi 等，2006）联系起来，如下所示。

Define $\mathcal{X}_t = \mathcal{S}_{1:t} \times \mathcal{A}_{1:t}$, and denote $\mathcal{L}_t^2$ as the space of square-integrable functions defined on $\mathcal{X}_t$ with respect to $P_t$.

定义 $\mathcal{X}_t = \mathcal{S}_{1:t} \times \mathcal{A}_{1:t}$,  并记  $\mathcal{L}_t^2$ 为在  $\mathcal{X}_t$  上、关于分布  $P_t$ 的**平方可积函数空间**。

---



Write \[ x_t := \{s_{1:t}, a_{1:t}\} \in \mathcal{X}_t, \quad \text{and} \quad y_t^* := R_t(s_{1:t+1}, a_{1:t}) + \max_{a_{t+1}} Q_{t+1}^*(s_{1:t+1}, a_{1:t}, a_{t+1}). \tag{5} \]

$Q_t^*$ can be regarded as the regression function of data $(x_t, y_t^*)$ and written as

则 $Q_t^*$ 可以被视为数据 $(x_t, y_t^*)$  的**回归函数**，并可表示为

 \[ Q_t^* = E[Y_t^* | X_t], \quad t = T, T-1, \dots, 1. \tag{6} \] 

Therefore, the standard approach in statistical learning theory (Györfi et al. 2006) yields 

因此，根据统计学习理论中的标准方法（Györfi 等，2006），可得

\[ Q_t^* = \arg\min_{Q_t \in \mathcal{L}_t^2} E\left[ \left(Y_t^* - Q_t(X_t)\right)^2 \right], \quad t = T, T-1, \dots, 1, \tag{7} \]

showing that optimal $Q$-functions can be obtained by solving T least squares problems. 

这表明，最优的 Q 函数可以通过求解  **T 个最小二乘问题**  来获得。



As shown  in Figure 1, through the iterative generation of input–output pairs, Q-learning can be regarded as  a series of supervised learning problems and incorporates four steps: hypothesis space selection,  optimization model development, Q-function training, and policy search. 

如图 1 所示，通过迭代生成输入–输出样本对，Q-learning 可以被视为**一系列监督学习问题**，并包含以下四个步骤：

1. **假设空间选择（hypothesis space selection）** 
2. **优化模型构建（optimization model development）**
3. **Q 函数训练（Q-function training）**
4. **策略搜索（policy search）**

The hypothesis space  selection step focuses on selecting a class of functions that encodes some a priori information  and determines the formats of Q-functions. 

The optimization model development step involves  mathematically defining the Q-functions via a sequence of optimization problems. 

After the  optimization model is developed, a feasible optimization algorithm should be designed in the  Q-function training step to derive Q-functions. 

Finally, the policy search step searches for the best  possible policies for choosing appropriate actions by maximizing the Q-functions.

**假设空间选择步骤**侧重于选择一类函数，该函数类能够编码一定的**先验信息**，并决定 $Q$ 函数的表示形式。 

**优化模型构建步骤**则通过一系列优化问题，从数学上定义 Q 函数。

在完成优化模型构建之后，**Q 函数训练步骤**需要设计一个可行的优化算法，用于求解并得到 Q 函数。

最后，**策略搜索步骤**通过**最大化 Q 函数**来寻找最优策略，从而选择合适的决策动作。

### 1.2. Problem setting

We are interested in Q-learning with T stages whose state space St is an infinite continuous space  and whose action space At is a discrete set of finite elements. 

我们关注的是一种具有 $T$ 个阶段的 Q-learning，其中状态空间 $S_t$ 为**无限连续空间**，而动作空间 $A_t$ 为**有限离散集合**。

To suit different chronic diseases, the  proposed Q-learning algorithm for DTRs should be feasible, efficient, and theoretically sound for a  large number of reward functions because different reward functions usually correspond to various  chronic diseases (Padmanabhan et al. 2017). The setting of Q-learning in this paper is shown in  Table 1.

为了适用于不同类型的慢性疾病，本文提出的用于动态治疗策略（DTRs）的 Q-learning 算法，应当在**大量奖励函数**下都具有**可行性、高效性以及理论可靠性**，因为不同的奖励函数通常对应不同的慢性疾病（Padmanabhan 等，2017）。本文所采用的 Q-learning 设置如表 1 所示。

As the starting point of Q-learning, hypothesis spaces regulate the format and properties of  the Q-functions to be learned and determine the type of optimization models to be developed.  

Linear spaces (Murphy 2005b) and deep neural networks (Fran ̧cois-Lavet et al. 2018) are widely  known hypothesis spaces for Q-learning. 

作为 Q-learning 的起点，**假设空间**用于规范待学习 Q 函数的形式和性质，并决定需要构建的优化模型类型。在线性空间（Murphy，2005b）和深度神经网络（François-Lavet 等，2018）是 Q-learning 中广泛使用的两类假设空间。

However, linear spaces have limited expressive power and cannot adapt to different reward functions, frequently performing poorly for chronic diseases, such  as cancer and sepsis (Yu et al. 2021, Sec.4), whereas deep neural networks lack solid theoretical  guarantees and are usually time consuming. 

然而，**线性空间的表达能力有限**，难以适应不同的奖励函数，在癌症、脓毒症等慢性疾病任务中通常表现较差（Yu 等，2021，第 4 节）；而**深度神经网络缺乏可靠的理论保证**，并且计算开销较大、耗时较长。

Since Q-learning for DTRs requires a perfect balance  between an expressive hypothesis space for quality guarantees and a simple hypothesis space for  scalability, we are faced with the following challenge:

由于用于 DTRs 的 Q-learning 需要在 **具有较强表达能力（以保证质量）** 和  **结构简单（以保证可扩展性）**  的假设空间之间取得平衡，因此我们面临如下挑战：

---

Problem 1. How can a hypothesis space be designed to equip Q-learning for DTRs to avoid  the limitations of both linear spaces and deep neural networks simultaneously?  

**问题 1：** 如何设计一种假设空间，使得用于 DTRs 的 Q-learning 能够同时避免**线性空间**和**深度神经网络**的局限性？

Once the hypothesis space is determined, optimization models are crucial in defining Q-functions.  In general, a good optimization model for Q-learning for DTRs should be solvable, scalable, and  interpretable. 

在确定假设空间之后，**优化模型**在定义 Q 函数中起着关键作用。一般而言，一个良好的用于 DTRs 的 Q-learning 优化模型应当具备**可解性、可扩展性和可解释性**。

- Solvability means that the developed model can be solved using standard optimization  algorithms like gradient descent.
-  **可解性（Solvability）**：指所构建的模型能够通过标准优化算法（如梯度下降）求解；
- Scalability implies that the cost of solving the optimization problem  is reasonable. 
- **可扩展性（Scalability）**：指求解优化问题的计算成本是可接受的；
- Interpretability means that the developed model is easy to understand. 
- **可解释性（Interpretability）**：指所构建的模型易于理解。
- Optimization  models with good solvability, scalability, and interpretability can be easily developed for linear  hypothesis spaces, but for nonlinear spaces, these properties are difficult to achieve. Therefore, we  focus on the following problem:
- 对于**线性假设空间**，通常可以较容易地构建同时具备可解性、可扩展性和可解释性的优化模型；但对于**非线性空间**，要同时满足这些性质则较为困难。因此，我们关注以下问题：

---

Problem 2. How to develop solvable, scalable, and interpretable optimization models for Qlearning for DTRs to optimize the performance of chosen nonlinear or infinite hypothesis spaces?  

**问题 2：** 如何为用于 DTRs 的 Q-learning 构建**可解、可扩展且具有可解释性**的优化模型，以优化所选择的**非线性或无限假设空间**的性能？

The solutions to Problems 1 and 2 are sufficient to develop DTRs through Q-learning. With the  emphasis on the quality guarantee of Q-learning for DTRs, we ultimately focus on the following  problem:

解决问题 1 和问题 2 即可通过 Q-learning 构建动态治疗策略（DTRs）。在进一步强调用于 DTRs 的 Q-learning 的**质量保证**时，我们最终关注以下问题：

---

Problem 3. How can the quality of the resulting DTRs be theoretically measured and how can  solid theoretical guarantees be provided for it?

**问题 3：** 如何从理论上衡量所得到的动态治疗策略（DTRs）的质量，并为其提供**可靠的理论保证**？

Unlike other applications, such as recommendations and supply chain management, which use  regret (Liu and Su 2022) to measure the quality of the developed policies, DTRs focus on the final  treatment outcomes, regardless of the median treatment processes, to fully explore the values of the  initial states. 

与推荐系统和供应链管理等其他应用不同，这些领域通常使用**后悔值（regret）**（Liu 和 Su，2022）来衡量所得到策略的质量，而动态治疗策略（DTRs）更关注**最终治疗结果**，而不是中间治疗过程，从而充分评估初始状态的价值。

This means that new analysis tools are required for theoretical guarantees.

这意味着需要新的分析工具来提供相应的**理论保证**。

### 1.3. Our contributions

We develop a scalable Q-learning algorithm based on kernel ridge regression (KRR) (Caponnetto  and De Vito 2007) for DTRs to address the above problems. Our main contributions are as follows:



- Methodological novelty: Breaking down Q-learning into T least squares regression problems,  we combine distributed learning (Zhang et al. 2015) and KRR to estimate Q-functions. ==Specifically==,  we adopt distributed regularized least squares over specific RKHS as the optimization model in  Q-learning. This approach avoids the quality limitations of linear Q-learning and addresses the  computational efficiency issues of deep Q-learning, simultaneously.

**方法学创新：**  通过将 Q-learning 分解为  T  个最小二乘回归问题，我们结合  **分布式学习** （Zhang 等，2015）与 **核岭回归（KRR）**  来估计 Q 函数。具体而言，在 Q-learning 中，我们采用 **特定再生核希尔伯特空间（RKHS）上的分布式正则化最小二乘** 作为优化模型。

该方法能够同时避免 **线性 Q-learning 的质量受限问题** ，并解决 **深度 Q-learning 的计算效率问题** 。

---

Theoretical Novelty: We propose a novel integral operator approach to analyze the generalization  error of Q-learning. Our theoretical results indicate that distributed learning does not increase the  generalization error, provided the data are not divided into too many subsets. 

**理论创新：** 我们提出一种新的**积分算子（integral operator）方法**来分析 Q-learning 的泛化误差。理论结果表明，只要数据没有被划分为过多子集，**分布式学习不会增加泛化误差**。

Additionally, the  generalization error bounds we derived are independent of the input dimensions, indicating that  the proposed algorithm scales well with respect to the dimensionality of the problem.

此外，我们推导得到的**泛化误差上界与输入维度无关**，这表明所提出的算法在问题维度增加时仍具有良好的可扩展性。

---

Numerical Novelty: We evaluate the proposed algorithm against linear Q-learning and several  state-of-the-art deep RL methods in two types of clinical trials for cancer treatments. Our numerical  results show that the performance of our approach is consistently better than that of linear Qlearning and comparable to that of deep RL methods. 

**数值实验创新：** 我们在两类癌症治疗的临床试验场景中，将所提出的算法与**线性 Q-learning**以及若干**最先进的深度强化学习方法**进行了对比评估。数值结果表明，所提出方法的性能始终优于线性 Q-learning，并且与深度强化学习方法具有**可比的性能表现**。

In terms of computational costs, our approach  requires significantly less training time than deep RL methods. These results demonstrate the  effectiveness and efficiency of the proposed kernel-based distributed Q-learning for DTRs.

在计算成本方面，我们的方法相比深度强化学习方法所需的**训练时间显著更少**。这些结果表明，所提出的**基于核函数的分布式 Q-learning 方法**在用于动态治疗策略（DTRs）时具有良好的**有效性和高效性**。

## 2. Related Work and Comparisons

EHRs have been widely used in health care decisions regarding chronic diseases over the last decade.  Q-learning is a promising approach to utilizing EHRs to yield high-quality DTRs and has gained  fruitful clinical effects in improving the quality of health care decisions (Tsiatis et al. 2019, Yu  et al. 2021). In particular, Zhao et al. (2009) used support vector regression (SVR) and extremely  randomized trees to fit approximated Q-functions for agent dosage decision-making in chemotherapy;  Humphrey (2017) employed classification and regression trees (CART), random forests, and a  modified version of multivariate adaptive regression splines to estimate Q-values for an advanced  generic cancer trial; Tseng et al. (2017) presented a multicomponent deep RL framework for patients  with NSCLC in which a deep Q-network (DQN) maps states into potential dose schemes to optimize  future radiotherapy outcomes; Raghu et al. (2017) proposed a fully connected dueling double DQN  approach to learn an approximation of the optimal action–value function for sepsis treatment. The  reader is referred to Tables III and IV of (Yu et al. 2021) for more related work on Q-learning for  DTRs.

在过去十年中，电子健康记录（EHRs）已被广泛应用于慢性疾病的医疗决策中。Q-learning 是一种有前景的方法，可利用 EHRs 生成高质量的动态治疗方案（DTRs），并在提升医疗决策质量方面取得了显著的临床效果（Tsiatis et al. 2019，Yu et al. 2021）。

具体而言，Zhao et al.（2009）使用支持向量回归（SVR）和极端随机树来拟合近似的 Q 函数，用于化疗中的剂量决策；Humphrey（2017）采用分类与回归树（CART）、随机森林以及一种改进的多元自适应回归样条方法，用于估计一个高级通用癌症试验中的 Q 值；Tseng et al.（2017）提出了一种用于非小细胞肺癌（NSCLC）患者的多组件深度强化学习框架，其中深度 Q 网络（DQN）将状态映射为潜在的剂量方案，以优化未来放疗效果；Raghu et al.（2017）提出了一种全连接的对抗双重 DQN 方法，用于学习败血症治疗中最优动作价值函数的近似表示。

关于 Q-learning 在动态治疗方案（DTRs）中的更多相关工作，读者可参考 Yu et al.（2021）的表 III 和表 IV。

Compared with these methods, our approach possesses three main novelties. First, we employ  kernel-based Q-learning to generate DTRs because the design of kernel methods satisfies the high  requirements of Q-learning for DTRs (the hypothesis space of a kernel method is large enough, and  the derived estimator is in a linear space). Second, we focus on distributed Q-learning algorithms  for DTRs that scale well according to the data size. Such a scalable variant of Q-learning for DTRs  has not been considered in the literature. Finally, we provide solid theoretical guarantees for the  proposed Q-learning approach in the framework of statistical learning theory (Murphy 2005b),  which is beyond the scope of the above mentioned papers.

与这些方法相比，我们的方法具有三方面的主要创新。

首先，我们采用基于核方法的 Q-learning 来生成动态治疗方案（DTRs），因为核方法的设计能够满足 Q-learning 在 DTRs 应用中的高要求（核方法的假设空间足够大，且所得到的估计量位于线性空间中）。

其次，我们重点研究用于 DTRs 的分布式 Q-learning 算法，使其能够随着数据规模的增长而良好扩展。而在现有文献中，尚未有针对 DTRs 的这种可扩展 Q-learning 变体。

最后，我们在统计学习理论框架下（Murphy 2005b）为所提出的 Q-learning 方法提供了严格的理论保证，这一点超出了上述相关工作的研究范围。

Note that there are also asynchronous parallel deep Q-learning algorithms, such as the distributed  method proposed by Ong et al. (2015), which can accelerate computations through distributed asynchronous gradient calculations. However, our method differs significantly. Specifically, their  Q-function remains consistent across different stages, whereas ours is stage-dependent. Regarding  communication, their methods entail costs per parameter iteration, while ours involves only one  communication per stage. More importantly, their distributed nodes require a real environment to  collect experiences, but ours trains solely on historical data without a real environment model.

需要注意的是，也存在一些异步并行的深度 Q-learning 算法，例如 Ong et al.（2015）提出的分布式方法，该方法可以通过分布式异步梯度计算来加速计算过程。

然而，我们的方法在本质上存在显著差异。具体而言，他们的 Q 函数在不同阶段保持一致，而我们的方法是阶段依赖的（stage-dependent）。

在通信开销方面，他们的方法在每次参数迭代时都需要通信，而我们的方法每个阶段仅需进行一次通信。

更重要的是，他们的分布式节点需要依赖真实环境来收集经验，而我们的方法完全基于历史数据进行训练，不需要真实环境模型。

## 6. Conclusion

This paper introduced a distributed Q-learning algorithm that integrates DKRR with Q-learning  to mitigate the computational burden associated with DTRs. Through the development of a novel  integral operator, we established tight generalization error bounds for the proposed algorithm within  the framework of statistical learning theory. Our theoretical findings indicate that the distributed  operator does not compromise the generalization error, provided that the data are not excessively  fragmented into numerous subsets. Furthermore, the derived error bounds are independent of the  data dimension, implying that the proposed algorithm scales effectively with respect to the problem’s  dimensions. In addition to our theoretical analysis, we also applied the proposed algorithm to two  types of clinical trials for cancer treatment, including those aimed at increasing survival time and  CSP. The numerical results verify our theoretical assertions and demonstrate the feasibility and  efficiency of the proposed algorithm in applications.

本文提出了一种将分布式核岭回归（DKRR）与 Q-learning 相结合的分布式 Q-learning 算法，以缓解动态治疗方案（DTRs）所面临的计算负担。通过构建一种新的积分算子，我们在统计学习理论框架下为所提出的算法建立了紧致的泛化误差界。理论结果表明，只要数据未被过度划分为过多子集，分布式算子不会损害模型的泛化性能。此外，所推导的误差界与数据维度无关，这意味着该算法在问题维度增加时仍具有良好的可扩展性。

除理论分析外，我们还将所提出的算法应用于两类癌症治疗临床试验，包括以提高患者生存时间和临床症状缓解概率（CSP）为目标的试验。数值实验结果验证了我们的理论结论，并表明该算法在实际应用中具有良好的可行性与高效性。
