输入：x | B,L,D

输出：future | B,P,d

步骤：

1. $x\in \mathbb{R}^{B\times L \times D} \gets enc\_embedding(x,x\_mark)$  
2. $\text{Multi-Scale \ future \ Extraction}$   
   1. $x\_S1 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L \times d} \gets x$ 
   2. $x\_S2 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/2 \times d} \gets \text{maxpool}(x)$  
   3. $x\_S3 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/4 \times d} \gets \text{maxpool}(x\_S2)$ 
   4. $x\_S4 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/8 \times d} \gets \text{maxpool}(x\_S3)$  
3. $\text{GADT}$ 
   1. $x\_AddNorm1 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L \times d}  \gets \text{GADTModel1}(x)$ 
   2. $x\_AddNorm2 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/2 \times d}  \gets \text{GADTModel2}(x\_S2)$  
   3. $x\_AddNorm3 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/4 \times d}  \gets \text{GADTModel3}(x\_S3)$ 
   4. $x\_AddNorm4 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/8 \times d}  \gets \text{GADTModel4}(x\_S4)$  
4. $\text{CrossSCM}$ 
   1. $x\_up4 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/4 \times d} \gets   \text{interpolate}(x\_ AddNorm4) $ 
   2. $ x\_cross3 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/4 \times d} \gets \text{cross\_attn3} (x\_up4,x\_AddNorm3,x\_AddNorm3) $  
   3. $x\_fused3 \in \mathbb R^{B \times L/4 \times d}  \gets\text{norm\_f3}(x\_AddNorm3 + x\_cross3) $  
   4. $x\_up3 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/2 \times d} \gets   \text{interpolate}(x\_fused3) $ 
   5. $ x\_cross2 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L/2 \times d} \gets \text{cross\_attn2} (x\_up3,x\_AddNorm2,x\_AddNorm2) $ 
   6. $x\_fused2 \in \mathbb R^{B \times L/2 \times d}  \gets\text{norm\_f2}(x\_AddNorm2 + x\_cross2) $
   7. $x\_up2 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L \times d} \gets   \text{interpolate}(x\_fused2) $ 
   8. $ x\_cross1 \in \mathbb R^{B\times L \times d} \gets \text{cross\_attn1} (x\_up2,x\_AddNorm1,x\_AddNorm1) $ 
   9. $x\_fused1 \in \mathbb R^{B \times L \times d}  \gets\text{norm\_f1}(x\_AddNorm1 + x\_cross1) $ 

5. $\text{HierCAD}$
   1. $pred\_4 \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times P/2 \times d } \gets \text{predictor\_4} (x\_AddNorm4)$ 
   2. $context\_3 \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times( L/4 + P/2) \times d } \gets \text{cat}(x\_fused3,pred\_4)$ 
   3. $pred\_3 \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times L/4 \times d } \gets \text{predictor\_3} (context\_3)$   
   4. $context\_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times( L/2 + P/2 + P/4) \times d } \gets \text{cat}(x\_fused2,pred\_4,pred\_3)$  
   5. $pred\_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times P/8 \times d } \gets \text{predictor\_2} (context\_2)$ 
   6. $context\_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times( L + P/2 + P/4 + P/8) \times d } \gets \text{cat}(x\_fused1,pred\_4,pred\_3,pred\_2)$ 
   7. $pred\_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times P/8 \times d } \gets \text{predictor\_1} (context\_1)$
   8. $prediction \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times P \times d } \gets \text{cat}(pred\_4,pred\_3,pred\_2,pred_1)$
6. $\text{Proj \& DeNorm} $
   1. $y\in \mathbb{R}^{B \times P \times D} \gets \text{Proj \& DeNorm}  (prediction)$
